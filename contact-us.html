Website questions and corrections

To report a broken link, factual error or unclear explanation, open an issue in the website’s GitHub repository. Include the page URL, the specific text in question and a reliable public source that supports the correction.

This is a public issue tracker. Do not include personal, financial, medical or insurance-account information. We do not provide policy-specific advice or guarantee a response time.

Questions about a Progressive policy or bill

For payments, billing disputes, cancellation notices, refunds, automatic-payment changes, coverage confirmation or any other account-specific matter, contact Progressive directly. This independent guide cannot view or change a policy, accept payments, stop drafts or make coverage decisions.

Visit Progressive’s official Contact Us page

You can also use Progressive Manage Your Policy for official account services. Verify contact details on Progressive’s website or your policy documents before sharing account information.

Protect your information

Do not post or send your policy number, Social Security number, date of birth, payment-card number, bank credentials, password, one-time passcode or insurance documents to this independent publisher or in a public issue. Share sensitive information only through a verified, secure channel provided by Progressive or the appropriate regulator.
