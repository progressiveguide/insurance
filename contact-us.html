For Progressive policy payments, billing disputes, cancellation questions, automatic-payment changes, refunds or coverage confirmation, contact the insurer directly through its official Contact Us page or sign in through Progressive Manage Your Policy.

What we can and cannot help with

We can explain general billing terms, help you identify which documents to review, and suggest questions to ask the insurer. We cannot view or change a policy, accept payment, negotiate a binding payment arrangement, stop a bank draft, approve a refund, verify active coverage or make an underwriting decision. Only Progressive or the applicable authorized insurer/agent channel can confirm account-specific outcomes.

Before contacting us

Please do not provide your full policy number, Social Security number, date of birth, payment-card number, bank credentials, password, one-time passcode or copies of insurance documents to this independent guide. If you need to discuss a specific account, use a verified secure channel provided by the insurer.

For editorial corrections, include the page URL, the exact statement you believe is incorrect and a public authoritative source. Do not include personal account details.

Official insurer contact

Progressive’s official contact page provides current options for phone, chat, email and account self-service. Progressive states that its general customer-service line is open 24/7. Contact methods and availability can vary by product and account, so follow the current official page.
