# Progressive Consumer Guide — website package

## Files
- `index.html` — full-length Progressive insurance billing guide and interactive tools
- `about-us.html` — publisher identity, purpose and independence disclosure
- `editorial-review.html` — editorial standards, sourcing and corrections
- `privacy-policy.html` — current website privacy disclosure (review against actual hosting before launch)
- `contact-us.html` — independent publisher contact and official Progressive contact route
- `assets/site.css` — shared responsive design stylesheet
- `sitemap.xml` — sitemap template
- `robots.txt` — crawler rules template

## Before publishing
1. Replace every `progressiveguide.github.io/insurance` placeholder with your real domain (including canonical URLs, JSON-LD, sitemap and robots.txt).
2. Confirm that `1 (856) 900-7834` is the correct number you intend to publish and that you are authorized to receive calls on it. It is clearly labelled as independent guide assistance, not Progressive.
3. Confirm that your actual hosting, logging, analytics, advertising and contact practices match `privacy-policy.html`; update it before adding any tracking, forms, chat widgets or uploads.
4. Review all legal disclosures and footer details for the publisher's actual legal identity and jurisdiction.
5. Upload the contents of this folder to the domain's public web root (for example, `public_html` on Hostinger). Keep the `assets` folder intact.
6. After the domain is live, submit `https://progressiveguide.github.io/insurance/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.

## Important
This is an independent informational site, not Progressive's official website or an authorized service center. Do not describe the independent phone line as Progressive billing, payment assistance, dispute department or an official hotline. Account actions and coverage confirmation must go through Progressive or the applicable authorized insurer/agent channel.
