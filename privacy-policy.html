Overview

This privacy notice describes the information practices of this informational website as currently designed. The site provides articles and links, including click-to-call links. It does not currently provide a website account, payment checkout, document-upload facility or online callback form. If those features are added, this notice must be reviewed and updated before launch.

This website is independent and is not operated by Progressive. When you follow a link to Progressive or another third-party website, that organization’s privacy policy and practices apply to your activity there.

Information this website receives

As currently configured, the site does not ask visitors to submit policy numbers, names, addresses, payment details, bank credentials or insurance documents through a website form. A click on a tel: link opens the visitor’s own phone application; the call is handled by the telephone service and the person or organization receiving the call, not by this webpage.

Web hosting providers may automatically process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure a website, such as IP address, request time, browser or device information, requested page and error logs. Whether and how long such logs are retained depends on the hosting provider and its configuration. The site owner should review the host’s current privacy and log-retention terms.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current page package does not intentionally install third-party analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral advertising scripts or marketing cookies. The hosting platform, security layer or future integrations may use essential technical storage or cookies. Before adding analytics, advertising, chat, forms, embedded media or consent tools, the publisher should update this notice to identify the provider, purpose, data categories, retention and available controls.

How to protect your information

  • Do not send passwords, one-time passcodes, full card numbers, bank login credentials or identity documents to this independent guide.
  • Use Progressive’s official website or app for account-specific billing and policy actions.
  • Verify a phone number independently before sharing account information.
  • Use secure, official channels when an insurer or regulator requests supporting documents.

Third-party links

Links to Progressive, NAIC, state regulators and other resources are provided for convenience and consumer education. We do not control those sites, their security, content, privacy practices or availability. Review the applicable third-party privacy notice before submitting information.

Children and sensitive information

This website is general consumer information and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please do not submit sensitive personal, financial, health or insurance information through ordinary email or an unverified channel.

Changes and contact

This notice is dated November 7, 2026. It should be updated when the website’s data practices, hosting configuration, analytics, advertising or interactive features change. For questions about this website’s privacy notice, use the Contact Us page. This page is a general disclosure template and should be reviewed for the publisher’s actual hosting, legal entity and jurisdiction before production publication.
