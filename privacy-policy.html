Overview

Progressive Consumer Guide is an independent informational website. This policy explains the website’s current practices as understood by its publisher. It is not a statement on behalf of Progressive or the hosting provider.

The site does not provide insurance services and does not need your policy or payment information to display its general educational content. Please do not send sensitive information to us.

Information and hosting

This site does not currently offer a form for submitting policy numbers, payment details, passwords, insurance documents or other account information. If you contact us through the public GitHub issue tracker linked from our Contact Us page, your post may be visible to the public and processed by GitHub under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not include personal or confidential details in an issue.

Like most websites, the hosting provider and related infrastructure may process technical data when serving or protecting pages. Depending on the provider’s configuration, this can include an IP address, request time, browser or device details, and the page requested. The provider controls its own logs, retention and security practices; consult the hosting provider’s current privacy information for details.

Cookies and analytics

The site’s current pages do not intentionally use advertising pixels, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics scripts. Hosting, security, or linked services may use technical storage or cookies as part of their operation. Browser settings can usually be used to review or clear cookies and limit their use, although some site or security features may not work as expected.

Third-party links

This website links to external services and information, including Progressive, GitHub, and government or insurance resources. When you visit another site, that service’s terms, privacy policy and data practices apply. We do not control third-party content or how those services collect and use information. Check that you are using an official website before entering account details.

Your choices and precautions

  • Do not post or send policy numbers, Social Security numbers, passwords, one-time passcodes, payment-card or bank details, or insurance documents to this site or its public issue tracker.
  • Use Progressive’s official website or app for policy, billing and account actions.
  • Review your browser’s privacy settings and the privacy notices of any third-party service you choose to use.
  • If you believe you shared sensitive information in a public post, remove it if possible and contact the relevant service directly.

Changes and contact

This policy may be revised if the site’s features, hosting arrangements or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. For questions or corrections about this website, use the contact options described on our Contact Us page. Do not use that route for policy or account support.
