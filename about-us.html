Our purpose

Insurance bills, payment histories and policy notices can be difficult to interpret. This website offers plain-language educational material to help readers understand common billing concepts, identify relevant documents and prepare questions for their insurer.

Our content is for general information only. It is not legal, financial or insurance advice, and it cannot determine what applies to a particular policy or claim.

Independent information

Progressive Consumer Guide is an independent publisher. It is not Progressive Insurance, is not affiliated with or endorsed by Progressive, and does not represent the insurer. References to Progressive and links to its website are provided to help readers reach official information.

Only the insurer can confirm policy terms, account status, payment posting, coverage, refunds or cancellation details. For account-specific help, use Progressive’s official contact page or its verified account-service channels.

What this guide can and cannot do

We can explain general billing terminology and point readers toward documents and official resources that may help answer their questions. We cannot access or change an insurance account, accept payments, make coverage decisions, resolve a billing dispute or provide a binding interpretation of a policy.

Do not send this website sensitive personal, financial or insurance-account information. Verify important information directly with your insurer.

Corrections and feedback

If you find a factual error, broken link or unclear explanation, see our Contact Us page for how to report it. Please do not include personal account details.

For information about how content is reviewed, visit Editorial Review.
